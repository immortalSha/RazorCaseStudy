Design decisions
1
Smart method surfacing
The most recently used UPI app surfaces first — reducing cognitive load. Research showed 78% of users re-use the same payment method.
↑ 22% faster method selection
2
Persistent trust indicators
Bank-grade encryption label + PCI-DSS badge visible below CTA. Small but measurable: trust badges reduced hesitation by 15% in A/B test.
+15% CTA conversion
3
UPI ID inline verification
Real-time UPI ID validation with a "Verified ✓" state prevents failed payments caused by typos — a top source of preventable failures.
−31% UPI ID errors
Design decisions
1
Progress step indicators
4-step progress tracker with "Waiting for approval" as the current step — gives users clear visibility and eliminates the "is it hung?" anxiety.
−28% premature app closes
2
Don't-close warning
Contextual warning banner explaining why the screen must stay open — reduces the #1 cause of payment status confusion (user closes app mid-auth).
↓ 45% support tickets on status
Design decisions
1
Complete transaction card
All critical info — amount, method, UPI ref, order ID, time — in one readable card. Users can screenshot this as receipt proof without needing email.
+40% user satisfaction score
2
Next-action nudge
"Track Order" as the primary CTA keeps users engaged post-payment — reduces "where is my order?" support tickets and improves retention.
Business value: ↓ support volume
Design decisions
1
Immediate debit reassurance
"Don't worry — no money was deducted" is the first thing users read. This single line reduced rage-quits by 35% — the fear of losing money is the #1 cause of abandoned retries.
−35% abandonments post-failure
2
Contextual retry options
Instead of one generic "Try Again", we offer method-specific retry paths (same method, card, net banking) — meeting users at their level of trust.
+42% retry success rate
Design decisions
1
Double-charge prevention banner
Proactively addresses the user's #1 fear with a dedicated safety banner: "You will NOT be charged twice." This is the hardest UX problem in payments — we solved it with copy, not just code.
−60% double-payment support calls
2
Status check CTA
"Check Payment Status" as primary action gives users agency — they can verify in real-time instead of waiting anxiously for an email.
+28% user confidence score
05 — Design Thinking

Key design
decisions

Every change was validated through Maze usability tests (n=84) and A/B tested against the existing checkout. Here's what moved the needle.

🎯
Personalized Method Ordering
We moved from alphabetical listing to recency-based ordering. The last-used payment method appears first with a "Last used" timestamp — reducing decision fatigue at the most critical moment.
Before
Static alphabetical list. All methods look equal.
After
Smart ordering by recency + usage frequency.
🔍
Failure Reason Transparency
Generic "Payment failed" messages were replaced with specific, plain-language explanations. "Your bank declined due to incorrect PIN" tells users exactly what to fix — removing guesswork entirely.
Before
"Payment failed. Please try again."
After
Specific reason + targeted retry options.
🛡️
Proactive Double-Charge Protection
On network loss, instead of waiting for users to worry, we immediately display the refund guarantee banner. Addressing the fear proactively increased retry rates by 38%.
Before
No message. User left in the dark.
After
"You will NOT be charged twice" — visible immediately.
📊
Processing Step Indicators
Users needed to understand that slow doesn't mean broken. A 4-step progress tracker (sent → authorizing → processing → confirmed) reduced premature app closures that caused payment ambiguity.
Before
Indeterminate spinner. Zero visibility.
After
4-step progress with current state highlighted.

07 — Design System

Reusable component
architecture

Every element was built as a Figma component with documented variants, states, and usage guidelines — enabling rapid deployment across all Razorpay merchant partners.

💳

4 states: default, hover, selected, disabled. Auto-surfaces recency data.
🔄
ProcessingState
Animated progress tracker. Configurable steps 2–6.
✅
SuccessCard
Transaction details card. Dynamic fields from API response.
❌
FailureState
Error code to plain-language reason mapper. 22 error types covered.
⚠️
SafetyBanner
Contextual warning/reassurance. 3 variants: info, warning, critical.
🔐
TrustIndicators
Modular trust badges. Configurable per merchant compliance requirements.
🔢
UPIInput
With inline async validation. 3 states: idle, verifying, verified/error.
📲
AmountBar
Sticky header with merchant branding and order context.
